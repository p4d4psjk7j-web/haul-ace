FAQ

Good questions.

How do I get a price?

Fill out the quote form below or email photos of your items with your ZIP code. Tell us about stairs, long carries or tight access. We reply with a written scope and price.

Will the price change when you show up?

Only if the job is different from the photos, and never without your OK. We walk the scope with you on arrival, and any change is approved before we start loading.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For curbside or garage pickups we can work while you're out, as long as access is arranged ahead of time. We'll send photos when we're done.

Where does my junk go?

Everything goes to an authorized disposal facility. When you'd like, usable items can go to donation if a local charity accepts them.

Do you work with realtors, landlords and contractors?

Yes. We offer move-out cleanouts, contractor punch-out cleanups and the Ace Closeout Pack with photos and a completion receipt. Ask about reserved route days for repeat work.

How do I pay?

Payment is collected when the job is complete and you've done the final walkthrough. We'll confirm accepted payment methods with your quote.
